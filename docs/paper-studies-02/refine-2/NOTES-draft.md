# Refine 2 — draft notes

Images in `drafts/before` and `drafts/after` are the same three views: flat sheet (workshop, step 0), folded display front, folded display back. Viewport 900×680, one Chromium session, camera positions matched (front `[0, 1.589, 5.271]`, flat `[0, 4.781, 3.053]`, back `[0, 1.589, -5.271]`). Headless Chromium with software WebGL. Not a phone and not a physical sheet. The reverse still does not rotate in this build, so these are not rotation sheets.

## Midnight orchard

The flat sheet and the folded front now show one brown limb. A short fork runs to the right-hand fruit, another runs down to the lower fruit, and a thin spur ends with no fruit. The separate curls from the before sheet are gone. The four fruits meet those endings. The gold one is wide, the lower apricot is lopsided, the right-hand persimmon is flatter, and the top apricot is smaller. Each has a darker lower tone instead of a pale round spot. Deep blue ground, the ochre reverse, and the empty lower right are unchanged. On the folded front the hem is still bare blue, and one fruit still sits in the skirt.

The persimmon is still the roundest fruit. The darker tone reads as a flat step across the lower half.

## Tidal bands

The bands are the same curves and the same order. At 0° the front is still pale, teal, cream, then the hem green, with the deep teal reverse in the collar. The pale band is slightly lighter than in the before front (the centre of that band reads about 179, 183, 167 rather than 166, 176, 160). Cream is only a step warmer. No extra band, outline, or wave mark.

Under the same lighting the pale band is still a little grey.

## Plum scatter

The before front is repeated petal clusters of similar shape. The after front has one large overlapping plum cluster on the bodice, a closed bud above it, a two-petal mark on the left of the skirt, a small coral mark on the right, and a more open coral cluster lower down. Blush ground separates them. The lower right and much of the skirt stay empty. The plum reverse is unchanged.

The same session’s folded fronts at 90°, 180°, and 270° (not saved here; the reverse still does not rotate) show the drift flipped at 180°, and at 90° and 270° one cluster with most of the skirt left blush. Those quieter turns were left quiet.

The lower coral cluster is still the most flower-like mark.

## Cut-paper mosaic

Unchanged. The after flat, front, and back match the before images. On the folded front the skirt is still mostly terracotta, with warm ground along the right side. The gaps between the fields read as separation, not as a sliver.

## Woven checks

The before front has a light strip along the blue bands and along the ochre bands. The after front is flat ochre and flat blue. At the crossings, a short step of the dark ground shows where one band passes under the other. Six bands still cross the sheet, and the check still reaches the hem. The slate reverse is the same colour as before; on the folded front the collar and sleeves stay that slate against the ochre and blue.

The interruptions are small and regular.

## Reverse garden

The before front is one mist colour. The after front has a single thin stem on the lower left, three leaves, and two buds. The centre and the right side of the dress stay mist. The stem is visible at the folded front scale.

On the back, the before collar is three separate ovals. The after collar is one sprig: a pale stem, a cropped tip at the top edge, a larger cream leaf, a bud, and a smaller green leaf, with stem visible between them. The warm blossoms remain in both sleeve areas. The flowering stem is still the main mark down the centre. Its leaves meet the stem, and the petals overlap. The amount of drawing on that stem is about the same as before.

The blossoms are still radial flowers. The sprig is small, because it sits in the top band of the sheet.
