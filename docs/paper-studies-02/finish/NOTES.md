# Finishing pass — what the sheets show

Captures are one Playwright session on system Chrome, software WebGL, viewport 900×680, of the production build. Every front used camera `[0, 1.589, 5.271]`. The Woven back used `[-0, 1.589, -5.271]`. Workshop and display controls are hidden after layout. The upper corners are room grey. The stand and the floor are in the frame. This is not a phone and not a physical sheet.

Before tiles are `873dbff`, kept at `archive/paper-studies-02-873dbff`. After tiles are this revision. Only the front drawings of Midnight orchard, Plum scatter, and Woven checks changed. Motifs were not moved to follow a turn.

## Midnight orchard

The before front is one thick limb with the fruit crowded against it, so the bodice reads as a solid crescent. The after front is a narrower limb. It starts with a rounded root, not a flat cut, and it tapers toward the tip. Four fruits hang on short stems: a large gold, a medium apricot to the lower left, a smaller persimmon, and a small apricot near the tip. Blue ground shows between the fruit and the wood. The hem under the branch stays blue. Deep blue ground and the ochre reverse are unchanged. No loose curls.

At 90° the branch sits high and the skirt is blue. At 180° it sits toward one side, still with blue below. At 270° it crosses the lower half. Those quieter skirts are the same drawing turned.

The limb is still fairly even through its middle. The smallest fruit is easy to miss at this size.

## Plum scatter

The before front’s main cluster is a sharp fan. The after front is one partly open blossom: five rounded petals, overlapping, with a shorter coral petal on the open side. The centre is the meeting of those petals, not a separate dot. A closed bud sits above, a small three-petal blossom sits to the lower right, one fallen coral petal sits to the left, and a small pair sits lower down. Blush ground stays open around them.

At 90° the blossom is high and to one side. At 180° and 270° it has turned, and much of the skirt stays blush. Those quieter turns were left quiet.

At dress size the blossom reads as one dark flower. The separate petal edges are softer than they are on the flat sheet, and the coral petal is the least distinct of the five.

## Woven checks

The over-under structure is the same: six ochre bands and six blue bands, one passing over the other. The dark ground between the bands is narrower, and the darker tone where a band goes under is closer to the band colour, so the colours read as continuous strips with a short break at each crossing. There is no light edge and no bevel. On this dress the ground gap at a crossing is about eleven pixels, against about fifteen before. The slate reverse drawing is unchanged. The back of the dress is still slate in the centre; the turned sides show the revised weave.

## What was not tested here

A real phone, mobile Safari, touch, or a physical sheet. The click-through is separate and was headless Chrome on the production preview.
